## 🔧 Installing a Gas Regulator

1. **Secure the gas cylinder** in an upright position using a chain or strap to prevent tipping.
2. **Remove the cylinder cap** carefully.
3. **Ensure the cylinder valve is fully closed** by turning **clockwise**.
4. **Inspect the valve outlet** for oil, dirt, or debris — clean with a lint-free cloth if necessary.
5. **Inspect the regulator and pressure gauges** for damage or contamination.
6. **Verify the CGA number** on the regulator matches the cylinder valve (e.g., CGA-350 for flammable gases).
7. **Check for a notch on the cylinder valve fitting**:
   - A **notch indicates a left-handed thread**, meaning it tightens **counterclockwise**.
8. **Ensure the outlet valve on the regulator is closed** (turn **clockwise** to close).
9. If the regulator **does not thread on smoothly**, stop — **you may have the wrong type** for the gas. **Never force it.**
10. **Hand-tighten the connection**, then use a **wrench** to fully secure it.

## 🔄 Pressurizing the System (Before Use)

1. **Turn the delivery pressure adjustment knob fully counterclockwise** to close it.
   - It should turn freely at the end, with **no resistance**.
2. **Stand beside or behind the regulator** — **never in front of the gauges**:
   - This is a **safety precaution** in case the regulator or gauges fail under pressure.
3. **Slowly open the cylinder valve (turn counterclockwise)** until the **inlet (high-pressure) gauge** shows cylinder pressure.
4. **Use a soap solution to check for leaks** at all joints and connections.
   - **Do not use Teflon tape** — CGA connections seal metal-to-metal.
5. **Close the cylinder valve (turn clockwise)** and observe whether pressure drops.
   - If it does, there is likely a leak.
6. If no leaks are present, **reopen the cylinder valve** (counterclockwise).
7. **Turn the delivery pressure adjustment knob clockwise** to set the desired output pressure.
8. **Open the regulator outlet valve (turn counterclockwise)** to start gas flow.

## ✅ After the Experiment

1. **Close the cylinder valve** (turn **clockwise**).
2. **Open the delivery pressure adjustment knob fully (turn clockwise)** to bleed out trapped gas.
3. **Open the regulator outlet valve (turn counterclockwise)** to release any downstream pressure.
4. Once gas has stopped flowing, **close the outlet valve (turn clockwise)**.
5. **Turn the delivery pressure knob fully counterclockwise** to reset it (turns freely at end).
6. **Ensure both gauges read zero**.
7. If needed, **remove the regulator**:
   - For CGA-350 (left-handed), turn the nut **clockwise** to loosen.
