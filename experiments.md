# Experiment Overview

Due to flow limitations in the T700 Mass Flow Controller — specifically, a maximum flow rate of only 0.01 SLPM — it will not be used in this experiment. Instead, we will carry out open sensor setup experiments by directly feeding gas from **Cylinder 01** at a regulated pressure of **30 psig**.

The cylinder contains **157.6 cubic feet** of gas at standard atmospheric conditions, equivalent to approximately **4,462 standard liters (SL)**. Based on an estimated delivery rate of **3 SLPM** — typical for 1/8" Teflon tubing at 35 psig — the gas supply is expected to last for approximately **1,487 minutes**, or **about 25 hours**. This provides a reliable timeframe for executing multiple experimental runs before cylinder depletion.

---

## Open Sensor Setup Experiments

### Experiment 1: Static Chamber Injection

**Objective:**  
Evaluate gas behavior and sensor response with the chamber **powered off** to prevent premature venting or leakage.

**Procedure:**
1. Begin data collection.
2. Open **Cylinder 01** and inject gas into the chamber at **30 psig** for **2 minutes**.
3. Continue monitoring and recording data.

**Manual Observations to Record:**
- ✅ Time the cylinder is opened  
- ✅ Time the cylinder is closed

---

### Experiment 2: Chamber at Room Temperature

**Objective:**  
Assess gas response with the chamber **powered on**, set to **room temperature and humidity conditions**:
- Temperature: **20 °C**
- Relative Humidity: **50%**

**Procedure:**
1. Configure the chamber to maintain standard temperature and humidity.
2. Open **Cylinder 01** and inject gas into the chamber at **30 psig** for **2 minutes**.
3. Start and continue data collection.

**Manual Observations to Record:**
- ✅ Time the cylinder is opened  
- ✅ Time the cylinder is closed
