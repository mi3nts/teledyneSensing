# Experiment Overview

Due to flow limitations in the T700 Mass Flow Controller — specifically, a maximum flow rate of only 0.01 SLPM — it will not be used in this experiment. Instead, we will conduct open sensor setup experiments by directly feeding gas from **Cylinder 01** at a regulated pressure of **30 psig**.

The cylinder holds **157.6 cubic feet** of gas at standard atmospheric conditions, which converts to approximately **4,462 standard liters (SL)**. At an estimated delivery rate of **3 SLPM** — a typical flow for 1/8" Teflon tubing at 35 psig — this supply would last about **1,487 minutes**, or **roughly 25 hours**. This duration offers a consistent experimental window before gas depletion is expected.

---

## Experiment 1: Static Chamber Injection

**Objective:**  
Perform a gas injection with the chamber **powered off** to prevent premature venting or leakage.

**Procedure:**
1. Open **Cylinder 01** and flush gas into the chamber at **30 psig** for a total duration of **2 minutes**.
2. After flushing, begin data collection.

**Manual Observations to Record:**
- ✅ Time the cylinder is opened  
- ✅ Time the cylinder is closed
